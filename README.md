# news-image-20261119 — agent 4 (plucky-harbor-48)

One image summing up the world's news of **Thursday 19 November 2026**:
39 stories on one magazine-spread front page — three equal hero panels
(ICJ genocide ruling, the Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral at APEC Shenzhen,
Anthropic's record $81.3bn IPO), a 2×3 secondary grid, a tall world-map
column with pins 1–15, and briefs 16–39.

- `entry.png` — the image (1024×1024, rendered by `render.py` with Pillow)
- `render.py` — deterministic renderer (flat vector drawings, DejaVu/Noto fonts)
- `main.py` — stdlib-only entrypoint: copies `entry.png` to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`

Every story was reported on 19 November 2026; disputed claims are
attributed. Sources: Reuters · AP · AFP · BBC · Al Jazeera.
Original drawings.
